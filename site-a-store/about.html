Our story

Celebrate simplicity.

Karbonn began in 2009 with one idea: the best of mobile technology should be easy to own and easy to use for every Indian.

Fifteen years and millions of smart decisions later, a new generation is about to discover its own Kamaal.

Part of India's mobile story

Made for how India talks, works and stays in touch.

Karbonn is a joint venture of New Delhi based Jaina Group and Bengaluru based UTL Group, led by Chairman Sudhir Hasija and Managing Director Pardeep Jain.

From regional language keyboards to service centres in small towns, Karbonn has always built for real, everyday life. Today the family spans smartphones, 4G feature phones, smart TVs and home appliances.
